New content pillar for @amourunjourglqmourtoujours: reposts of fashion models /
influencers with a vintage aesthetic, mixed into the existing 108-post schedule
to piggyback on their audience and the collab algorithm boost.

## Cadence (how it slots into the schedule)

- **Every 4th post is a Muse slot** (start ratio 1-in-4; move to 1-in-3 if the
  slots outperform the archive posts after 2–3 weeks).
- Alternate: **female muse → 3 brand posts → male muse → 3 brand posts → …**
- Muse slots go on the highest-engagement days observed so far (their audience
  spillover is worth the most there).
- On a 36-post cycle this adds ~9 muse slots per cycle.

## Permission first — this is the reach play, not just the safe play

Credit is not a license: a tag + "DM to remove" does not authorize a commercial
brand account to use someone's photo, and in France droit à l'image makes it
worse. The real operational risk is not a lawsuit — it's **copyright strikes**:
a few takedown reports and Instagram restricts or deletes the account, which
kills the reach project entirely.

But asking first is also simply the better growth tactic:

1. **DM before posting** (templates below). Micro/mid influencers say yes fast —
   a brand repost is free promo for them. Screenshot the yes, keep it in
   `permissions/`.
2. **Ask for a Collab tag, not a plain repost.** When they accept Instagram's
   collaborator invite, the post appears on both profiles and both audiences —
   that is the single biggest reach multiplier available, far beyond a repost.
3. **Gift a frame to get "with my eyewear" photos.** Never edit our eyewear
   onto someone's photo — a real person appearing to wear/endorse the product
   without their consent is fabricated endorsement and is the one thing that
   can't be walked back with a deletion. Instead: ship them a frame, they shoot
   it their way, we repost/collab. Note: Lexi Lou sells collabs on Collabstr
   (collabstr.com/lexilouthur), so she is reachable and open to exactly this —
   gifting or a small paid post gets an authentic "wearing L'Écurie" photo.
4. **No answer = no post.** Move to the next candidate; the pipeline below
   should always hold 6–10 pending names so slots never go empty.

Same logic applies (softer) to the archive images already in this repo
(Deneuve, Casta/YSL): agency-owned photos (Getty etc.) do get enforced against
commercial accounts. Vintage posts survive longest when framed as editorial
moodboard content, not product promotion, and are swapped out on any complaint.

## Caption template (muse slots)

```
[One line in French — mood, era, why she/he is a muse]
[One line in English]

en collaboration avec @handle 🤍
#amourunjour #vintagefashion #70sstyle #eyewear [+ 3–5 tags from their niche]
```

- Tag the creator **in the photo and as collaborator**, mention in caption.
- If they shot with a photographer, credit the photographer too (they will
  reshare — second audience for free).

## Takedown protocol

If anyone (creator, photographer, agency) objects: delete within the hour, no
argument, reply with an apology, log it in the tracker. Never repost that
creator again without written permission.
