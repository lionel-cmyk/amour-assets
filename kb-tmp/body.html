L’Écurie Paris is on the cover of Key Biscayne Magazine. For the August 2026 issue, photographed by Jorge Duva under the creative direction of Isa Zapata, Kate Svetla makes her grand entrance in an orange gown, gold at the wrist and the throat, and Lissa in Amber on her face.

Coral-stone steps, a black lacquered door, agaves in dark ceramic pots. The frame is a single fine line of gold-plated titanium, square and light, with lenses the colour of late afternoon. It asks for nothing, which is why the eye stays on it.

Inside, the Stylewise editorial Forever Summer moves to the water. A red halter swimsuit, a black pool float, a slice of watermelon, and Papillon in Rosé Coral, the rimless butterfly whose lenses fade from rose to clear. Miami light at that hour does the rest.

Both styles are made in Sabae, Japan, in titanium plated with 18K gold and inspected by hand. Lissa is the quieter of the two, a geometry that suits almost every face. Papillon is the statement, all lens and no rim.
